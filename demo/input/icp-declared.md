# Apify — declared target customer (from Apify's own public pages)

Apify calls itself "the largest marketplace of trusted tools for AI," built for teams that need real-time web data, competitor tracking, lead generation, and social monitoring (apify.com).

Declared use cases (31 listed) span: AI/LLM data feeds, e-commerce, product development, automation (web RPA), competitive intelligence, social media intelligence, market research, real estate + hospitality, lead generation, and sentiment analysis (apify.com/use-cases).

Named customer segments on the site: Startups, Universities, Nonprofits, and "Apify for Enterprise" (apify.com/pricing, apify.com/enterprise).

Enterprise positioning: "Accurate, reliable, and compliant web data for your business. From any website. At any scale," pitched at "the world's top data-driven teams," with custom SLAs, guaranteed data, and a dedicated team (apify.com/enterprise).

Pricing runs Free through Business (self-serve, pay-as-you-go compute) up to a Custom Enterprise tier with SSO and an account manager — a dual motion of solo developers/small teams on self-serve plans and larger data-driven organizations on Enterprise (apify.com/pricing).
